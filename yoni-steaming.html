About Yoni Steaming

Ancient Wisdom for Modern Women

Yoni steaming, also known as vaginal steaming, is a traditional practice used by women across cultures for centuries. This gentle treatment uses the power of herb-infused steam to support your intimate health and overall wellbeing.

At Tessa Bobir, we offer this sacred practice in a comfortable, private setting where you can relax and receive the healing benefits of carefully selected organic herbs.

Our Herb Blends

Organic Herbal Blends

We use only the highest quality organic herbs, carefully selected for their healing properties.

Cleansing (Reset)

  • • Postpartum/ fourth trimester
  • • Menstrual Cycles 28 Days or Longer
  • • Missing Periods
  • • Periods that are controlled with oral contraceptives
  • • Recovering from Miscarriage
  • • Detoxing and wishing to conceive

Cooling (Soothe)

  • • Hot flashes
  • • Night sweats
  • • Vaginal dryness
  • • Users living in hot climates
  • • Infections or viruses accompanied by vaginal dryness or burning
  • • People who have had a hysterectomy

Disinfecting (Balance)

Irregular discharge and infections accompanied with cervical mucus such as:

  • • Inflammation
  • • HPV
  • • STDs
  • • Viruses
  • • Yeast Infections
  • • Irregular Papsmears, Cervical Dysplasia, and Cancer

Gentle (Ease)

  • • Short Menstrual Cycles (27 days or less)
  • • Uterine fatigue
  • • Interim Bleeding (fresh spotting or spontaneous bleeding between periods)
  • • Fourth trimester- periods return before 9 months postpartum, heavy blood flow, or who have a history of short menstrual cycles

Your yoni steaming session takes place in a private, beautifully prepared room. You'll be seated comfortably on our specially designed steam throne, wrapped in soft blankets as the gentle herbal steam works its magic.

Important Information

When to Avoid Steaming

While yoni steaming is beneficial for many women, there are times when it should be avoided:

✗

During pregnancy

✗

During active menstruation (heavy flow days)

✗

With an active infection

✗

If you have an IUD (consult your practitioner)

We always conduct a thorough consultation before your first session to ensure yoni steaming is right for you.
