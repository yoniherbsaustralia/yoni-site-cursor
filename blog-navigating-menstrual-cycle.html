The Phases of Your Cycle

Learning your body's unique language.

📅 21 April 2026 ⏱️ 9 min read 🌖 Cycle Syncing

You Are Not a Linear Being

Most of the modern world operates on a constant, 24-hour cycle. We are expected to wake up every single day with the exact same level of energy, output, and sociability. However, as individuals with menstrual cycles, our biology does not work like this. We run on an approximately 28-day infradian rhythm.

When we ignore this biological reality and push through burnout month after month, we experience PMS, fatigue, and hormonal chaos. The secret to vitality is to stop fighting your cyclic nature and start viewing your cycle through the lens of the Four Seasons.

Two Synchronous Cycles

When we talk about the menstrual cycle, we are actually discussing two separate but synchronous events happening simultaneously: the Ovulatory Cycle (which governs the development and release of an egg from the ovaries) and the Uterine Cycle (which governs the thickening and shedding of the uterine lining).

To accurately understand your body's unique language, you must track your cycle starting from Day 1. Day 1 is exactly the first day you experience true, fresh red bleeding—not just pre-menstrual spotting. By consistently logging Day 1, you create an anchor point to map these tandem hormonal shifts and accurately predict your next phase.

The Four Inner Phases

  • Inner Winter (Menstruation): Day 1 to the end of bleeding. Hormones are at their lowest. This is your time to retreat. Prioritize deep rest, warm nourishing foods, and reflective journaling. Say no to social obligations without guilt.
  • Inner Spring (Follicular Phase): Directly after bleeding. Estrogen is rising. Energy begins to return. This is a brilliant time for brainstorming, starting new projects, light exercise, and tapping into fresh creativity.
  • Inner Summer (Ovulation): Estrogen and testosterone peak. You are likely feeling your most magnetic, sociable, and energetic. This is the optimal time for big conversations, heavy workouts, public speaking, and socializing.
  • Inner Autumn (Luteal Phase): The longest phase before bleeding. Progesterone rises, which has a calming effect. You may feel your energy turn inward. Detail-oriented tasks, wrapping up projects, and nesting are ideal here. As you approach the end of the phase, gracefully scale back your commitments.

The Power of Cycle Syncing

By mapping your cycle and adjusting your diet, exercise, and social calendar to match the hormonal season you are in, you afford your body profound grace. Cycle syncing leads to reduced PMS, smoother periods, and a much kinder relationship with your own body.
