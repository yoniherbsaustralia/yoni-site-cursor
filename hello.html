About

Welcome to my Practice

Hi there, I’m Tessa, Great grand daughter, Grand daughter, daughter, mother, sister, aunty, holistic massage therapist & Mother Earth Lover, and I’m deeply passionate about holistic wellness and grounded healing practices.

My journey into this work began when I started to reflect on this with a desire to create a safe, nurturing space for women to reconnect with their bodies, their cycles, and their innate wisdom.

My offerings are born out of my own lived experiences of young motherhood, learning the importance of taking responsibility of my own actions and the power that comes with accountability, I am who I am today because of self-reflection and intentionally implementing small every day changes into my life, little by little, a little becomes a lot, I believe in the power of being held by those who believe in transparency, integrity, compassion and gratitude. I have experienced what can happen through working on yourself alone, and working alongside suited (and even not suited) support people.

Whether through holistic massage, yoni steaming, or birth support, my approach is always heart-centred, trauma-informed, and tailored to you.
