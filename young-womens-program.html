Young Women's Program

Empowering young women to understand, honour, and celebrate their bodies through education, connection, and self-care practices.

About the Program

A Sacred Space for Growth

Our Young Women's Program creates a safe, nurturing environment where young women can explore their connection to their bodies, understand their menstrual cycles, and develop healthy self-care practices that will serve them throughout their lives.

Through guided workshops, circle sharing, and hands-on education, participants learn to embrace their femininity with confidence and grace. This program is designed to fill the gaps left by traditional education, offering wisdom that has been passed down through generations.

Program Features

What's Included

Cycle Wisdom

Learn to track and understand your menstrual cycle, recognising its phases and how to harness your energy throughout the month.

Self-Care Rituals

Discover nurturing practices including herbal remedies, gentle movement, and mindfulness techniques for your wellbeing.

Circle Sharing

Connect with other young women in a supportive circle, sharing experiences and building lasting friendships.

Who Is This For

Perfect for Young Women

This program is ideal for young women who are beginning their journey into womanhood and want to approach it with knowledge, confidence, and connection. Whether you've just started menstruating or have been on this journey for a few years, there's something valuable for everyone.

Parents and guardians are welcome to attend an information session before the program begins to understand what their daughters will be learning and how they can support them at home.

Groups are formed by age ranges, with programs catering to young women up to 19 years of age, ensuring each participant is learning alongside peers at a similar stage in their journey.
