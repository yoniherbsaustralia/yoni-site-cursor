About Kahuna Massage

Ancient Hawaiian Healing Art

Kahuna massage is a traditional Hawaiian healing practice that uses long, flowing, rhythmic strokes combined with breathwork and intention. This deeply transformative bodywork goes far beyond physical relaxation — it works to release emotional blockages and restore harmony within the whole being.

Each session is intuitively guided, honouring the connection between practitioner and client. The flowing movements mimic the ocean waves, creating a deeply meditative and nurturing experience that allows the body to let go and heal.

How Kahuna Massage Helps

Deep Relaxation

The long, flowing strokes and rhythmic movements induce a profoundly deep state of relaxation, calming the nervous system and reducing stress.

Emotional Release

Kahuna massage works on an energetic level to help release stored emotions, past trauma, and negative thought patterns held within the body.

Your Session

What to Expect

Your Kahuna massage experience begins with a brief consultation to understand your needs and intentions for the session. The treatment space is prepared with care, creating a warm and sacred environment for your healing journey.

Using forearms and hands, flowing continuous strokes move over the entire body, creating a wave-like rhythm that deeply soothes and restores. Warm organic oils are used to nourish your skin throughout the session.

FAQ

Common Questions

Kahuna massage uses long, continuous, flowing strokes that cover the entire body, rather than focusing on individual muscle groups. It incorporates breathwork, intention, and energetic healing, making it a holistic experience that addresses the physical, emotional, and spiritual body.
Sessions typically range from 60 to 90 minutes. Longer sessions allow for a deeper, more meditative experience and are recommended for those seeking profound relaxation and emotional release.
Kahuna massage is suitable for most people. However, we recommend discussing any health conditions, pregnancy, or concerns during your initial consultation so we can tailor the treatment to your needs.
You will be appropriately draped throughout the session for your comfort and privacy. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing to arrive in. All details will be discussed before your session begins.
