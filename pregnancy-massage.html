Pregnancy Massage

Gentle, nurturing massage tailored for each trimester, providing comfort and relaxation throughout your beautiful pregnancy journey.

About Pregnancy Massage

Nurturing Care for You & Baby

Pregnancy is a beautiful journey, but it can also bring physical discomforts. Our specialised pregnancy massage provides safe, gentle relief for the aches and tensions that come with your changing body.

Using pregnancy-safe techniques and positioning, we help you find comfort and relaxation while supporting your body as it nurtures new life. Each session is tailored to your current trimester and specific needs.

Your Journey

Massage for Every Trimester

First Trimester

Gentle, relaxing techniques focusing on stress relief and supporting your body through early pregnancy changes. Light pressure and nurturing touch.

Second Trimester

Address growing belly comfort, lower back strain, and hip tension. Side-lying positions ensure complete comfort for you and baby.

Third Trimester

Focus on preparing your body for birth, relieving sciatic discomfort, and reducing swelling. Deeply relaxing and restorative.

Your Safety

Safe & Comfortable

Your safety and comfort are our absolute priority. All our pregnancy massage practitioners are specially trained in prenatal techniques and understand the unique needs of pregnant bodies.

We use specially designed cushioning systems that allow you to lie comfortably in various positions, taking pressure off your belly and lower back. Only pregnancy-safe oils and products are used during your treatment.
