Matriarch Massage

Experience deeply nurturing massage to support and honour your body's transition through peri and post menopause.

Nurturing Your Transition

Supporting Your Journey

Peri and post menopause are significant transitions in a woman's life. It is a time when the body requires deep nourishment, care, and understanding. Our matriarch massage provides a sacred, safe space to reconnect with your shifting body, relieve physical discomforts, and calm the nervous system during this profound change.

Through intentional touch and nurturing warmth, we honour your experience, helping you to find ease, emotional balance, and physical comfort as you navigate the different stages of menopause.

Benefits

How Matriarch Massage Helps

Deep Relaxation

Massage helps to lower cortisol levels and soothe the nervous system, offering a peaceful retreat from the physical and emotional fluctuations of menopause.

Emotional Support

Creating a nurturing container for your journey, touch therapy provides profound emotional support, helping to release held tension and foster self-compassion.

Physical Relief

Gentle, flowing strokes help ease the common muscle stiffness and joint aches that can arise during this transition, promoting greater comfort and mobility.

Your Session

What to Expect

Your session begins with a warm consultation to understand your current experiences, symptoms, and needs. We tailor each session to honour exactly where you are in your peri or post menopausal journey.
