About Fertility Massage

Support Your Conception Journey

Fertility massage is a gentle, non-invasive therapy that focuses on the reproductive system and surrounding areas. This specialised treatment aims to improve blood flow to the uterus and ovaries, support hormonal balance, and create optimal conditions for conception.

Whether you're trying to conceive naturally, preparing for IVF, or simply want to support your reproductive health, our fertility massage provides nurturing care that honours your body's wisdom.

Benefits

How Fertility Massage Helps

Reproductive Health

Gentle abdominal massage helps position the uterus optimally and improves circulation to all reproductive organs.

Stress Relief

Deep relaxation techniques help reduce cortisol levels, which can interfere with reproductive hormones and conception.

Hormonal Balance

Supporting the body's natural hormone production and regulation through improved lymphatic flow and relaxation.

Your Session

What to Expect

Your fertility massage session begins with a consultation where we discuss your health history, fertility journey, and any specific concerns. This allows us to tailor the treatment to your unique needs.

The massage itself combines gentle abdominal massage with relaxing full-body techniques. We use premium organic oils infused with herbs traditionally used to support fertility.
